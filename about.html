Our Story

Built around quality, presentation, and hospitality.

Proper Shot was created with one goal: to make event coffee feel just as considered as every other detail of the occasion.

For us, great coffee is only part of the experience. Every detail matters — from the quality of the espresso and the presentation of the cart to the way each guest is welcomed and served.

We combine thoughtfully crafted drinks, professional service, and a clean, elevated setup so the coffee bar feels like a natural part of the event rather than an afterthought.

Whether it’s a wedding, a company gathering, or a private celebration, our goal is simple: serve excellent drinks and create a moment your guests genuinely enjoy.
